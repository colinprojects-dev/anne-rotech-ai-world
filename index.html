How the shortlist was chosen

Practical customer experience, business-process decisions, organizational readiness, training and implementation learning carry the most weight. Procurement remains distinct from inventory, logistics, planning, maintenance and product-lifecycle work. Specific Finance and HCM processes remain conditional until discovery confirms their relevance. Healthcare examples are compared on process and operating context rather than industry label alone.

Advanced AI architecture, model development, agent-building labs, database administration, infrastructure, sales and marketing content, and unrelated industry material were screened out. Core customer stories may mention AI or future innovation; their relevance explanations identify the process, training or implementation lesson. One optional introductory AI session is retained and can be omitted.

Gaps and practical limits

  • No retained session establishes a complete, beginner-level walkthrough of every Finance, Procurement/Supply Chain and HCM business process. Customer lessons and narrower functional guidance serve different purposes.
  • Changing people’s ways of working and product/engineering change management are distinct. The PLM customer panel is conditional on that actual functional scope.
  • No dedicated non-AI general implementation-peer roundtable was confirmed. The two scheduled functional Q&As have specific innovation scopes; customer speakers are not confirmed introductions.
  • The catalog does not provide a complete change-management methodology, a Rotech-specific training plan or proof that a particular customer’s design fits Rotech.
  • Sunday has limited core process content in this shortlist. Paid reporting or manager-workflow training is conditional and excludes the other full-day course.
  • Course fees, recording access, seat availability and measured walking times were not provided. BOO2220 asks participants to bring a laptop; laptops are not supplied.
  • Supplied background does not establish systems, module ownership, budgets, purchasing authority, Phase 1 status or next-phase scope. A completed Fusion ERP implementation is not assumed.
